# Before you copy a successful AI video

A two-minute originality check:

1. Write your own point of view in one sentence before choosing a thumbnail.
2. Pick one thing you actually tested, noticed, paid for, or got wrong that supports it.
3. Look at the title, thumbnail, examples, and ending. Keep useful conventions, but make the central example and conclusion yours.

4. Choose visual effects and sound cues that support your own delivery. Reuse a useful technique without copying every beat of someone else’s edit.

The same editing tools do not require the same message. Similar thumbnails alone do not prove copying; this short shows the host’s personal reaction to one search screenshot.
