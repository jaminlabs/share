# Taste profile, from "Everyone edits with Claude"

Lots of people edit video with Claude now. The cuts start to look alike because the AI only knows the generic
version of a good edit. To get your version, it has to know your taste.

`taste-profile.md` is a one-page form for that. Fill it in once, then give it to whatever AI edits for you (Claude
Code, Codex, a CapCut or Premiere agent, a human editor). Put it in the project instructions (for example
`CLAUDE.md`) or paste it at the top of every edit request.

## How to fill it in

1. Pick three of your own videos that did well and three you would never make again. Watch them before you write.
2. Fill in every line with what you actually do, not what sounds good. "Cut every pause over 0.3 s" helps.
   "Make it engaging" doesn't.
3. Add numbers wherever you can: words per minute, seconds before the first word, how long a card stays up.
4. Keep the "never" list short and strict. The editor follows those rules even when its own defaults say otherwise.
5. After every edit you have to fix by hand, add one line about the fix. After a month the profile describes you
   better than any prompt you could write from scratch.

## Why one page

A long style guide gets skimmed by people and by models. One page with concrete rules is easy to hold in context
on every edit, and it's easy to see what changed when you update it.

Nothing in here needs an account, a key or a paid tool.
