## Three questions to ask about the next compute headline
1. Who signed with whom? Find the company announcement, not a logo diagram.
2. Is this installed capacity, contracted future capacity, or an expression of interest?
3. Is the claim a measured result or the speaker's prediction?

Musk's neocloud shakeout is a prediction, not a tally of failed firms. [His original X reply, October 25 UTC](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2114434646914068862) is truncated in the logged-out embed before the neocloud sentence. [Digg's reporting](https://digg.com/tech/u0excy9k) attributes the more-than-200 count and failure/consolidation forecast to Musk; the count has not been independently audited here.
