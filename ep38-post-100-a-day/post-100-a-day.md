# Posting 100 a day: the rule, the math, my loop

## The rule, as said
- Gary Vaynerchuk, 2018 presentation in Auckland, quoted on garyvaynerchuk.com (July 2, 2019): "If you are not producing
  100 pieces of content … every single day you are leaving the greatest opportunity in the world on the table." Same
  talk: "start posting a hundred pieces of content a day. Maybe 52 of those are just single tweets, maybe 26 of them
  are Instagram stories."
- Alex Hormozi, "The Rule of 100" (The Game, Ep 248, Nov 10, 2020): "if you do 100 of something every day, you'll
  likely get better at it", with 100 reach-outs or $100 of ads a day as the examples. I found no line of his telling
  people to post 100 times a day.

## The math
- You don't film 100 videos. You film long and slice it.
- 2 to 3 hours of talking a day is 120 to 180 minutes. A 30-second Short uses about 30 seconds of it, so there is
  room for 100. Most of those minutes won't be good enough, so 10 good Shorts a day is the honest target.
- What breaks first is the hand work: finding the good parts, cutting them, captions, and uploading each one to
  YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads.

## My loop (CueRoom plus my PC)
1. Record once on the phone with CueRoom.
2. CueRoom reads the take and lists the lines worth redoing (0 to 3), with the reason. I retake only those lines.
3. I put the take in the edit queue. My PC cuts the Shorts and checks them; the app shows which step it is on.
4. I watch the finished cut. Nothing posts until I approve it.
5. After I approve the YouTube upload, the same Short is scheduled to X, Threads, Instagram and TikTok.

Example from my log on Oct 28, 2026: one Short went public on YouTube at 10:00, then X at 10:10, Threads at 10:20,
Instagram at 10:30 (TikTok had gone out the day before).

## Copy it without my tools
- One long recording a day, on a fixed time.
- A list of the weak lines right after recording, and you redo only those.
- One place that holds the cuts until you approve them.
- One approval that schedules every platform.
