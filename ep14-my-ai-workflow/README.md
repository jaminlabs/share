# One task before another AI tool

Video, transcript and numbers: [jaminlabs.com/v/my-ai-setup](https://jaminlabs.com/v/my-ai-setup/?src=github)

Use this worksheet to test one recurring task with a tool you already have. Allow about ten minutes to write the brief; the task itself may take longer.

| Field | Write your answer |
|---|---|
| Task | What specific job should be finished? |
| Input | Which files or source material does it need? |
| Output | What should I be able to open or use? |
| Check | How will I check whether the output is useful and correct? |
| Boundary | What must it leave alone, and when must it ask me? |

## Example: a research brief

- Task: Find one recent development relevant to my next video.
- Input: One topic, two public sources, and a date range.
- Output: At most one candidate with a direct source link, the relevant detail, and why it matters to my audience. Zero candidates is acceptable.
- Check: Open the source and verify the main claim. Decide whether it gives me something worth explaining.
- Boundary: Research only. Do not publish or contact anyone.

## Reusable instruction

“Use the input listed here to produce the output listed here. Keep source facts separate from your interpretation. State what you could not verify. Do not fill the list with weak results. Show how you checked the result, and stop before the actions listed under Boundary.”

This is a newly written planning worksheet based on the creator's workflow. It is not an exported bot configuration or a tested unattended system. No software purchase is required by this worksheet. Results depend on the task, sources, and review.
