# Why a Photoshop knockoff works but an AI app copy won't (EP39)

Photoshop came out in February 1990. Its core tools haven't moved much in a long time (that part is my take; Adobe did
add Generative Fill in 2023). Because the original stands still, a copy of it is good enough: Photopea, a free
editor that runs in your browser and opens PSD files, is the example on screen.

An AI app is different. If it does the whole Photoshop job for you and understands what you're going for, it has to
keep learning, and a copy made today is behind next week. My guess: the AI products that last are the ones that keep
getting better. The ones that stall for a week or two fade, and someone ships a knockoff.

Anything you can build in one or two days, someone else can build in one or two days too.

## Take this: a five-minute copy test for your AI product

`hard-to-copy-check.md` is a list of ten questions to ask about something you're building or thinking of buying.
Each one is a yes or no. Count the yeses at the end: it tells you whether a weekend clone could replace it, and which
part you'd need to work on.
