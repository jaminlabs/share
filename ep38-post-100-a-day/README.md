# Gary Vee says post 100 a day (EP38)

Gary Vaynerchuk's line is "If you are not producing 100 pieces of content … every single day you are leaving the
greatest opportunity in the world on the table." It's from a 2018 presentation in Auckland, quoted on his own site:
https://garyvaynerchuk.com/create-content-100-pieces-per-day/ . His pieces include single tweets and stories, mostly
cut from longer content.

I said Alex Hormozi says it too. I couldn't find him saying "post 100 a day". His "Rule of 100" is doing 100 of your
main action every day, like 100 reach-outs or $100 a day in ads ("The Rule of 100", The Game, Ep 248, Nov 2020).

Nobody films 100 separate videos a day. You record long and cut it up: two or three hours of talking a day could give
you 100 Shorts, or maybe 10 good ones. That only works if the cutting and the posting aren't done by hand.

## Take this

`post-100-a-day.md`: the rule with its real sources, the math from one recording to many Shorts, and the loop I run
with my app CueRoom (record once, retake only the weak lines, the PC edits, it posts once I approve). Copy the loop
with whatever tools you use.
