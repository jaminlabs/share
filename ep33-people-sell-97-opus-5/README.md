# Before you pay $97 for an AI video editing course

From the jamin labs Short "People sell $97 courses on Opus 5.5 video editing".

Opus 5.5 came out on Sep 22, 2026. By Oct 12, Kim Doyal
[wrote](https://kimdoyal.substack.com/p/how-to-edit-video-with-claude-even) that one AI YouTuber's Opus 5.5 video
tool was only available through his $97-a-month paid community. Free, open-source editors for Claude are on GitHub
too.

What's here:

- [`before-you-pay.md`](before-you-pay.md): five questions to answer before you pay for a course, and three free
  open-source Claude video editors to try first (all MIT licensed, checked on GitHub on Oct 27, 2026).

What you still pay for either way: a Claude plan. Opus is not on the Free plan; Pro is $20 a month billed monthly
([claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), read Oct 27, 2026).
