# Build a research feed you can actually use

Start with one topic, two sources, and one review per day. This is a small experiment, not a guarantee of accurate research.

1. Fill in `research-brief.txt` with your topic, exclusions, and the decision the research should help you make.
2. Give it to an agent that can browse. Ask it to save the source URLs, observation times, and exact evidence with each finding.
3. Open the original sources before acting. Mark one finding useful and one irrelevant, then explain why in the next run.

Keep these checks separate:

- **Execution:** did the job finish and save a fresh result?
- **Evidence:** do the source and quoted numbers support the claim?
- **Usefulness:** did the finding help you choose an action?

A usefulness rating is not a factual accuracy percentage. A cumulative view count is not growth: growth requires two comparable observations of the same item. Never interpret a missing result as proof that interest has declined.

The accompanying video demonstrates a personal dashboard captured on October 18, 2026. It shows an ongoing experiment, not measured time savings or customer results. The template below is a simplified adaptation of that workflow, not a ready-to-run copy of the full system.

No service, API key, or subscription is required to read or adapt this template. Your chosen research tool may have its own costs and limits.
