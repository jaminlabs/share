# Read an AI data-center power claim in one minute

**Start with four questions:** Is this a target or operating capacity? Is the number IT load, whole-site load, or generation capacity? What year is it for? Where does the electricity come from?

**Power is a rate; energy is the accumulated amount.** One gigawatt equals 1,000 megawatts. A hypothetical 100 MW site running continuously at that load for 24 hours uses 2,400 MWh (100 × 24), or 2.4 GWh. At a constant 1 GW for a full 365-day year, that is 8.76 TWh. These are unit-conversion examples, not measured use by a named facility; actual utilization, cooling and losses matter. Do not compare a GW capacity announcement directly with annual TWh production.

**What Musk actually targeted:** At SpaceX's October 20, 2026 webcast he described an additional 10 GW of compute coming online in 2027. That is guidance, not verified operating capacity, and not a promise to manufacture 10 GW of turbines every year. The same call describes casting terrestrial turbine blades and vanes using SpaceX's rocket-engine casting capability to shorten the power-supply bottleneck. [Primary webcast transcript, PDF pp3 and 10](https://s21.q4cdn.com/184289198/files/content_files/SpaceX-Third-Quarter-2026-Webcast-Transcript.pdf).

**Where the power can come from:** The grid supplies an energy mix. On-site natural-gas turbines burn fuel to generate electricity while avoiding a wait for a larger grid connection. This does not make them carbon-free: fuel combustion emits CO2. EIA emphasizes that emissions per kWh vary with fuel and plant efficiency. [EIA electricity CO2 FAQ](https://www.eia.gov/tools/faqs/faq.php?id=74&t=11).

**A permit is location-specific:** SELC's July 16, 2025 appeal concerned the July 2 permit for 15 gas turbines in South Memphis, Tennessee. Its allegations should be attributed. A July 30, 2026 company update concerns Southaven, Mississippi: it describes removing mobile turbines and replacing their supply with a permitted permanent plant. These are distinct sites and dates. [SELC permit appeal](https://www.selc.org/press-release/groups-appeal-permit-for-xais-south-memphis-data-center-decisions-around-unpermitted-methane-gas-turbines/), [company update](https://x.ai/memphis/updates).

**Use this checklist:** Write down capacity, measurement boundary, utilization, year, fuel mix, permit location, and whether an independent source has confirmed operation. A dramatic plume in a video cannot by itself tell you the pollutant concentration or establish compliance.

Sources checked October 26, 2026. This note does not estimate a facility's actual emissions or predict whether its targets will be achieved.
