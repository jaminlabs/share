# EP40: Don't buy the AI course

Short from jamin labs (October 2026). My take: most AI courses go stale fast because someone open-sources the same
thing for free. Put the money into the AI subscription you use every day and build something that is hard to copy.

## What's in here
- `spend-this-instead.md`: a check to run before you pay for an AI course, where to put the money instead, and a
  prompt to paste into your AI subscription so it teaches you the thing by building it with you.
